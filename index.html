    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I calculate daily protein intake for gym workouts? ▼
    For resistance training and hypertrophy (muscle growth), research recommends consuming between 1.6g and 2.2g of protein per kilogram of body weight. For general wellness and sedentary lifestyle, 0.8g to 1.0g per kg is sufficient.
    What are the best vegetarian high-protein sources? ▼
    Soya chunks are among the highest vegetarian sources with roughly 52g protein per 100g unbaked. Other solid options include paneer (18g/100g), curd/greek yogurt, boiled chana, moong sprouts, lentils (dal), and whey protein.
    Does eating more protein automatically build muscle? ▼
    Protein provides the necessary amino acids (building blocks), but progressive overload resistance training is required to stimulate muscle synthesis. Unused excess calories will be burned as energy or stored as body fat.
    Can I track custom foods that are not in the preset list? ▼
    Yes! Use the Custom Food Entry section above to enter any dish and its exact protein content in grams. It will be added to your live daily total immediately.

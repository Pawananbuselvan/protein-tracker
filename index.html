Why does protein requirement change?

Sedentary bodies only require 0.8g per kg of body weight for vital organ and cell turnover. When lifting weights, your muscles sustain micro-tears requiring 1.6 to 2.0g per kg to trigger Muscle Protein Synthesis (MPS) and repair stronger.
